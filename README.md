UC6: Booking History & Reporting

Goal: Maintain a complete audit trail of confirmed reservations.

Description:  
Confirmed bookings are stored in a history list for long-term tracking. 
Admin can review past reservations, cancel bookings, and generate reports 
(total bookings, breakdown by room type). This supports customer service, 
audit requirements, and management reporting. UC6 ensures that the hotel has 
a reliable record of all transactions, enabling accountability, simplified 
reporting, and improved customer support. It mirrors real-world hotel systems 
where historical data is critical for audits, analytics, and service recovery.
