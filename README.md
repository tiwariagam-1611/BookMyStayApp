UC2: Guest Search & Availability
Goal: Provide Guests with transparent access to room availability and pricing.
Description:  
Guests can browse the current inventory, view all available room types, and check specific categories for availability 
and price. This empowers guests to make informed decisions before placing a booking request. UC2 simulates the browsing 
stage of hotel booking, where customers explore options online or at the front desk. It builds trust and transparency by 
showing real-time availability directly from the inventory.
